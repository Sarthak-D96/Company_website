========================================================================
META (FACEBOOK) DOMAIN & BUSINESS VERIFICATION INSTRUCTIONS
For SUNVALOR TECHNOLOGIES LLP
========================================================================

To enable features like WhatsApp Business API, Facebook Ads targeting, and organic post attribution, you must verify ownership of your domain in the Meta Business Manager.

Below are the 3 standard verification methods. Choose the ONE that is most convenient for your setup.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 1: Meta-tag Verification (Recommended for fast static deployment)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Meta will provide you with a HTML meta tag that looks like this:
<meta name="facebook-domain-verification" content="abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz012345" />

How to apply:
1. Open the file: WEBSITE/index.html
2. Search for the text: "META BUSINESS DOMAIN VERIFICATION HOLDER"
3. Replace the content attribute value "YOUR_META_VERIFICATION_TOKEN_HERE" with your actual token code (e.g. "abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz012345").
4. Save the file and deploy it to your hosting platform (Vercel, Netlify, Firebase, etc.).
5. Go back to Meta Business Manager and click "Verify Domain".

------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 2: HTML File Upload
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Meta will ask you to download a verification file, typically named "something.html" (e.g., "meta12345abcdef.html"), and upload it to your website root.

How to apply:
1. Download the verification file from the Meta Business Manager.
2. Place this downloaded file directly inside your WEBSITE/ directory:
   WEBSITE/
   ├── [downloaded-meta-filename].html  <-- PUT IT HERE
   ├── index.html
   ├── privacy.html
   ├── terms.html
   ├── css/
   ├── js/
   └── assets/
3. Deploy the site.
4. Verify that you can access the page in your browser by visiting:
   https://yourdomain.com/[downloaded-meta-filename].html
5. Go back to Meta Business Manager and click "Verify Domain".

------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 3: DNS TXT Record
------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you do not want to change your code, you can verify via your domain registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare, Route 53, etc.).

How to apply:
1. Log in to your domain registrar account and navigate to the DNS Zone Editor / DNS Settings for your domain.
2. Add a new record with the following settings:
   - Type: TXT
   - Host / Name: @ (or leave blank if it represents the root domain)
   - TTL: 3600 (or default)
   - Value / Text: facebook-domain-verification=[your-verification-code]
3. Save the DNS record. (Note: DNS propagation can take from a few minutes up to 24 hours).
4. Go back to Meta Business Manager and click "Verify Domain".
